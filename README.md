# 💚 LIFE-LINK

### Your Health. Our Priority.

> **Care that stays close when it matters.**

LIFE-LINK is an **AI-powered healthcare assistance platform** designed to connect users with intelligent symptom guidance, emergency assistance, nearby healthcare facilities, multilingual interaction, and community-based blood support through one connected experience.

---

## 🌐 Overview

Healthcare situations can become confusing, especially when users need quick guidance or are unsure about what to do next.

**LIFE-LINK** aims to simplify that journey by bringing multiple healthcare assistance features together in one platform.

From describing symptoms to finding nearby hospitals and accessing emergency-related assistance, LIFE-LINK is designed around one simple idea:

> **Make healthcare assistance more accessible, connected, and easier to navigate.**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Symptom Triage

Users can describe their symptoms and receive AI-assisted guidance based on the information provided.

The system can help with:

- Symptom analysis
- Urgency-level guidance
- Relevant medical specialization
- Suggested next steps
- Emergency escalation when appropriate

> ⚠️ LIFE-LINK provides AI-assisted guidance and does not replace professional medical diagnosis.

---

### 🚨 Emergency Assistance

When serious symptoms are detected, LIFE-LINK can provide an emergency-oriented flow to help users quickly access relevant support.

Features include:

- Emergency Mode
- Emergency contacts
- Emergency guidance
- Nearby healthcare support

The goal is to reduce the time between recognizing a serious situation and finding appropriate assistance.

---

### 🏥 Nearby Hospital Discovery

LIFE-LINK helps users discover healthcare facilities around them.

Depending on the available services, users can access information such as:

- Nearby hospitals
- Hospital details
- Availability-related information
- Healthcare facility discovery
- Appointment-related functionality

---
